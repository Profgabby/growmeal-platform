# Crop growing guide template

1. Crop name, verified botanical identity, and local names.
2. Who can grow it (all participant pathways; age-appropriate task adaptations).
3. Where it grows (climate, season, sunlight, space and water).
4. What you need (locally available low-cost options; no purchase requirement).
5. Propagation and planting (seed/cutting/transplant, depth, spacing).
6. Water and soil care (qualitative until locally validated).
7. Daily/weekly observations and safe troubleshooting.
8. Harvest stages: germination, first edible harvest, mature harvest, productive lifespan.
9. Harvest hygiene, preparation and food-safety notes.
10. Record harvest and plan next crop.
11. Source links, reviewer, verification date, confidence and version.
